AI-Assisted Research Report: A counterexample to Scottish Book Problem 155
  • Author: Yoshito Ishiki
  • Version: 1.0
  • Date: 30 August 2026
  • Status: Publicly posted
  • License: CC BY 4.0
  • DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22168214

This report constructs real Banach spaces and a bijection that preserves all pairwise distances on every closed ball of radius 1/4 but is not a global isometry, providing a counterexample to Scottish Book Problem 155. OpenAI Codex assisted with mathematical exploration, literature searches, language editing, and LaTeX preparation. The author verified the arguments and assumes sole responsibility for the contents.
